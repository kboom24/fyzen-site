# Credits — Noodle Sprawl

A Playtoom original by Hexan. Slug: `noodle-path`.

- Code: original, written for Hexan (game.js, core.js, levels.js, art.js). Engine: Hexan Engine (`hexan.js`), original.
- Game design, levels and the solver/generator: original. Handcrafted levels were designed with the built-in solver; generated levels are seeded and solver-verified.
- Characters: Udo the noodle and the ice cubes are original characters, drawn procedurally on canvas at runtime. No image files.
- Sound effects and music: synthesized at runtime with WebAudio (`HX.Audio`) from original note patterns. No sample files.
- Fonts: platform brand fonts Nunito and Fredoka (Latin WOFF2 subsets, SIL Open Font License 1.1), bundled locally by the engine build into `brand/fonts/` with their licence texts; system UI fallback. Nothing is downloaded from outside.
- Third-party assets: none.
