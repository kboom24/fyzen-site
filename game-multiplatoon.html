Multiplatoon

Puzzle · Times-table crowd runner · Drag left or right

About Multiplatoon

Multiplatoon is a crowd-runner where your times tables are your weapon. You lead a squad of painted tin toy soldiers down a cardboard road laid across a bedroom floor, and every few steps the road splits into two cellophane gates propped up on pencils. Each gate shows a little sum: "4×6" adds twenty-four soldiers, "×2" doubles the whole squad, a red "−3×5" takes fifteen away and "÷2" halves you. Work out which side gives more, slide into it with one thumb and watch the platoon swell or shrink. A pop-up shows the worked fact ("4 × 6 = 24") every time, so the tables sink in while you play.

Further along, rival squads with spiky helmets block the road and clash with your soldiers one for one, spinning tops sweep across one side of the road, and at the end a cardboard castle waits with its own garrison. Bring a big enough army to topple it. There are fifteen levels across three worlds (Bedroom Floor, Playroom Rug and Attic at Night), each built around one times table from the 2s to the 12s, mixing in earlier tables as you go. Every level has three stars, shown on a goal card before you start, tracked live on screen and explained on the results screen: topple the castle, reach the target army size, and pick the bigger gate every time. The results screen also lists the facts you marched through.

The Daily March is a new seeded course every day, the same for everyone, with a streak to keep, and Endless March keeps building castles until your army runs out. Medals earned from stars and soldiers buy a bigger starting squad in the Barracks, and you can unlock eight uniforms, from Garden Guard green to Golden Parade. After every run you see your global rank, your percentile and the next army size to beat. If you are playing offline it is a clearly labelled preview against sample players. Share a picture card that reads "My army hit 2,048" or a challenge link that drops a friend into the same course with your army to beat. Progress, stars, medals and streaks are saved on your device. It plays in portrait or landscape with one thumb, a mouse or the arrow keys, and works offline.

How to play

  1. Steer. Drag left or right anywhere with one thumb, hold and drag with a mouse, or use the arrow keys or A and D.
  2. Pick the bigger gate. Each gate shows a times-table sum. Work out which side gives more soldiers and steer into it; the worked fact pops up every time.
  3. Topple the castle. Clash with rival squads, dodge spinning tops and bring a big enough army to topple the castle at the end of each level.
  4. Daily and Endless. The Daily March is the same new course for everyone each day, and Endless March keeps building castles until your army runs out.
