Lantern Nine

Real 9-ball pool under a warm lantern: true cue-ball physics, spin, four computer opponents, a daily rack and 18 trick shots.

EngineHexan.jsFytoon in-house engine
GenrePool · 9-ballSports
OrientationAnyPhone, tablet, desktop
Daily modeDaily rackNew one every day

Lantern Nine is a 9-ball pool game built around a real physics simulation. Balls slide, then roll, then stop the way they do on cloth; cushions rebound and grip; object balls throw a little when they are cut; and the spin you put on the cue ball matters. Touch the big cue ball in the corner to strike low for draw, high for follow or to the side for english, then pull the power slider and let go.

The rules are proper 9-ball. You must always hit the lowest-numbered ball first, and something has to reach a cushion after contact. Scratches, wrong first contact, missing every ball or knocking a ball off the table are fouls, and your opponent gets ball in hand anywhere on the table. Sink the 9 on any legal shot to win the rack, even early with a combination. Right after the break you may play a push-out, and three fouls in a row lose the rack.

Play the Computer against four opponents, from the friendly Rookie to the Lantern Master, who plays position, safeties, banks and combinations. The aim guide shows a ghost ball and where both balls will travel, and it gets shorter as the opponent gets stronger. Pass & Play lets two people share one device.

The Daily Rack gives everyone the same scattered table each day. Clear all nine balls in as few strokes as you can (each foul adds two) and as fast as you can, then send a challenge link so a friend plays exactly the same rack and sees a head-to-head result. Eighteen Trick Shots teach draw, follow, banks, combos, safeties and runouts. Each has a goal card and three stars, and the results screen explains every star you earned or missed.

Stars, wins and streaks unlock new cloth colours and cues in the Locker. Everything is saved in your browser on this device, and your player name is optional.
