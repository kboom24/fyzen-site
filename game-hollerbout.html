Hollerbout

A street-poster rhythm sing-off. Echo each rival's call on four big lanes, out-holler 6 original rivals and play a new Daily Bout song every day.

EngineHexan.jsFyzen in-house engine
GenreRhythm battleAction & Arcade
OrientationAnyPhone, tablet, desktop
Daily modeDaily BoutNew one every day

Hollerbout is a rhythm battle drawn like a wall of wheatpaste posters. Mox, a small tangerine bean with a big voice, wants to be the loudest act on the block. Every rival sings a short call; then it is your turn to echo it back. Sticker arrows slide toward four huge lanes, and you tap (or press the arrow keys or WASD) as each one lands on its chalk outline. Hit them clean and Mox sings the melody right back at them; miss and he bonks a note and the tug-of-war meter slides toward your rival.

Six original rivals hold court on six original songs, each one composed and synthesized live in your browser: Dumpling Dee in the laundromat, Coo Carl the tracksuit pigeon, Slurpa the disco noodle cup, Bricky the brick with the chain, Meter Maude the parking meter, and Gargo, the gargoyle boss of the rooftop. Beat a rival to unlock the next.

Four difficulty levels mean everyone can finish a song. Chill lets you tap anywhere on the screen, Easy gives you extra time, Normal is the real bout, and Hard speeds things up and adds chords. Gentle mode halves miss damage, note speed is adjustable, and a tap test sets your audio and touch delay so timing feels fair on any phone, tablet or laptop.

Every bout earns up to three stars, and the results screen explains each one: finish the song with health left, reach the accuracy goal, and play a full combo. You also get a letter grade, your judgement counts, a hint if you tend to tap early or late, and a preview of where your score would place on the global board. Stars, streaks and wins unlock hats, microphones and sticker styles for Mox in the Locker.

The Daily Bout writes a brand-new song every day, the same for everyone, and keeps your streak. Share a poster-style result card, or copy a beat-me link: your friend plays the same song and races your ghost score live. Everything saves in your browser, and a player name is optional.
