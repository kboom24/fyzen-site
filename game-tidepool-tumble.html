Tidepool Tumble

Drop cute tidepool critters into the pool. When two of the same critter touch, they merge into a bigger one. Chain merges and grow a Moonwhale!

EngineHexan.jsFyzen in-house engine
GenreDrop & mergePuzzle
OrientationAnyPhone, tablet, desktop
Daily modeNoneFree play only

Tidepool Tumble is a squishy physics merge game set in a sunny rock pool. Nimbo the cloud drifts above the water carrying the next critter. Drag, move the mouse or use the arrow keys to aim, then let go or press Space to drop it. When two of the same critter touch, they merge into the next one up, from a tiny Dewdrop through Bubblet, Snailet, Shrimplet, Puffball, Starbun, Crabbit, Jellybell, Octopal and Shellback, all the way to the legendary Moonwhale. Merge two Moonwhales for a Moonrise and a huge bonus.

Every critter wobbles, squashes when it lands, blinks, looks worried when the pool gets full and giggles when it merges. The physics are tuned so piles stack calmly and never jitter, so every chain reaction plays out exactly the way you set it up. Merge again within a moment of the last merge to build a chain: each link raises the multiplier from x1.5 up to x4, with big callouts, bursts of bubbles, screen shake and rising notes.

Scoring is simple and shown before you play: each merge is worth the points of the critter it creates, multiplied by your chain. If the pile stays above the dashed line for three seconds the tide goes out and the run ends. The line glows, a heartbeat plays and a countdown appears, and dropping back under in time earns a Near Miss bonus. The results screen breaks down your points by critter, shows every goal you earned or missed and lists the critters you found, and it waits for you.

Each Tide Level sets three goals, such as a score, a critter to reach and a chain length. Two stars in one run clears the level. Discoveries fill your Collection Book with each critter's name, art and a little story, and progress unlocks new pools such as Coral Cove, Kelp Forest, Moonlit Lagoon and the Glowing Abyss, plus critter skins like Pastel Pals, Candy Stripes and Night Glow. The Daily Drop gives everyone the same sequence each day, with your best score and a day streak. Share a result card and send a challenge link: your friend gets the exact same drops and tries to beat your score.
