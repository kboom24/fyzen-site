Hopabet

A daily word-hop puzzle: change one letter at a time, land on theme words, and reach the Word of the Day before your hops run out.

GenreWord puzzlePuzzle
Best onAny screenPhone, tablet, or computer
Daily funWord of the DayA new one every day

Hopabet is a bite-sized word puzzle with a new challenge every day. Pip the pond frog sits on a lily pad with a four-letter word. Your job is to hop, one letter at a time, from that start word to today's Word of the Day. Every hop changes exactly one letter, and every word you land on has to be a real word, so HARE can become HIRE, then HIDE, then TIDE, and so on.

The twist is the daily theme. Each puzzle has a theme such as Animals, Weather, Food or At Home, and some of the words along the way belong to it. Land on a theme word and a lotus blooms: that is a find. Reaching the Word of the Day earns your first star, and taking a scenic route through enough theme words earns the second and third stars. You have a hop budget, so the puzzle is about planning: the shortest route is safe, but the best score comes from detours that collect finds and still arrive in time.

Scoring is simple and shown before you start: 500 points for reaching the Word of the Day, 150 per find, 25 per spare hop, minus 150 for each hint. The goal card lists exactly what each star needs, and the results screen explains which stars you earned and why. Undo is free, the on-screen keyboard lights up the letters that make a real word, and a short animated demo teaches the rules in seconds.

The Daily Hop is the same for every player on the same date and keeps a streak going. When you finish, share a spoiler-free result card made of little lily pads, lotus flowers and a star, plus a challenge link that opens the same puzzle for a friend and shows your score to beat. Between dailies, Practice mode has unlimited levels that grow from three-hop warmups to longer routes, with ranks from Tadpole to Grand Hopabet and unlockable pad skins such as Sakura, Sunset, Moonlit, Candy and Golden.

Every answer, theme word and hint comes from a curated, family-friendly word list, and the game plays fully offline with progress saved on your device.
