Chromacomb

Drop stacks of hex tiles, let matching colors hop together and burst 10 of a kind into chain-reaction cascades.

EngineHexan.jsFizzSoup in-house engine
GenreColor sortPuzzle
OrientationAnyPhone, tablet, desktop
Daily modeNoneFree play only

Chromacomb is a calm-but-explosive color sorting puzzle played on a honeycomb. Each turn you get three stacks of colored hex tiles. Drag one onto any empty cell (or tap a stack, then tap a cell). Every neighbor whose top color matches hops over, tile by tile, onto the stack that tidies the board best. When ten or more tiles of one color sit on top of a stack, they burst in a shower of sparks, uncovering the colors underneath, which can merge again and set off a chain reaction. A good placement can trigger merge after burst after merge, with rising notes, combo counters and screen-shaking finales.

There is no move limit: you only lose when the comb fills up, so every placement is a little planning puzzle. Thirty hand-tuned Levels take you across ten board shapes, from the small Hive to the Halo ring, Blossom, Starburst and Twin Pools. Each level has one clear goal (clear tiles, clear a color, or reach a score) and three stars: one for the goal, one for finishing in few placements, and one for a high score or a big combo. A results screen explains every star you earned or missed, and nothing moves on until you choose.

The Daily Board gives everyone the same seeded board and tray each day, with a streak to keep. Endless mode keeps adding colors as your score climbs and saves your best. Two boosters help out of tight spots: Shuffle rerolls the tray and Hammer smashes one stack. Stars and streaks unlock six original color palettes. Progress, stars, boosters, palettes and best scores are saved on your device.
