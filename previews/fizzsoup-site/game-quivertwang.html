Quivertwang

A best-of-3 ragdoll archery duel in woodblock-print style. Drag back to draw, let go to shoot, and knock wobbly archers off their pillars.

Quivertwang is a ragdoll archery duel printed in woodcut ink. Two floppy archers face off on stone pillars above a carved sea. Drag back anywhere to draw your bow, let go to shoot, and watch the arrow arc through the wind. Every hit knocks the body around for real: a shot to the arm spins your foe, a headshot does double damage, and the final arrow plays out in slow motion with a big K.O. stamp. There is no blood, only splashes of printer's ink.

Both archers shoot whenever their bow is ready, so duels are fast and frantic. Crates and planks give cover until they splinter, the wind changes every round, and arrows can even knock each other out of the air. Special arrows join your quiver as you climb: Split fires three at once, Ice freezes your foe in place, Bounce ricochets off wood, and Tug lassos your foe with a rope and yanks them toward you, sometimes right off their pillar for a ring out.

Climb the Boss Ladder through twelve original archers, from Sprig the Squire to the Woodcut King. Each boss has a goal card and three stars: win the duel, win without dropping a round, and finish a round with a headshot. Quick Duel lets you pick an Easy, Normal, Hard or Brutal opponent, and 2 Players puts two people on one phone, tablet or keyboard at the same time. The Daily Duel gives everyone the same foe, arena and wind each day and keeps your streak.

After every duel you see your score, a preview of your global standing, and a share card with your slow-motion finishing shot plus a challenge link that replays the exact same duel for a friend. Feathers, stars, wins and headshots unlock bows and hats in the Locker. Progress is saved in your browser on this device.
