Monster evolve .io · Action & Arcade

Morphmaw.io

Eat snacks, bite smaller monsters and evolve your sticker beast through a branching tree. Out-chomp the bots and reach form 12!

GenreMonster evolve .ioAction & Arcade
Best onAny screenPhone, tablet, or computer
Daily funDaily ArenaA new one every day

Morphmaw.io is an eat-to-evolve monster brawler drawn as glossy die-cut stickers on doodled notebook pages. You start as Blip, a tiny one-eyed blob. Gobble the snacks scattered across the page to fill your DNA bar, bite monsters smaller than you, and run from the big ones. Every monster wears a coloured ring: green means prey, yellow means an even fight, red means run. Red arrows at the edge of the screen warn you when something hungry is closing in, and bushes let you hide.

When your DNA bar is full you curl up in a safe cocoon and choose your next form from two or three cards. There are three lineages and 34 forms in all. Chomp monsters lunge forward with a big bite, Spike monsters spin and hit everything around them, and Puff monsters spit slowing globs from range. You can stay in your line or branch into a neighbouring one, so every run grows into a different creature, from Nibbit and Prickle to the form-12 apex beasts. Reaching form 12 earns a special share card.

Six arenas (Margin Meadow, Sticky Desk, Crayon Canyon, Blueprint Bay, Eraser Peaks and Sketchbook Summit) each have a goal card and three stars: reach a target form, knock out a number of monsters in one run, and climb to number one on the food chain. Earning a star opens the next page. The Daily Arena gives everyone the same map and a twist such as Snack Storm or Giant Day, and keeps your streak. The Evolution Codex records every form you discover, and stars, KOs and discoveries unlock sticker finishes like Glitter, Holo Shine and Gold Foil.

After each run you see the stars you earned and missed, your DNA score, a clearly labelled preview of your global standing, and a share card with your evolution path plus a challenge link that loads the same arena and rivals for a friend. Rivals are offline bots, so it plays anywhere with no account. Progress is saved in your browser on this device.

Step 1MoveDrag anywhere with one thumb, steer with the mouse pointer, or use WASD or the arrow keys.
Step 2Eat and growGobble snacks and bite monsters with a green ring. Yellow is an even fight, red means run.
Step 3EvolveWhen the DNA bar is full, pick your next form from the cards: Chomp, Spike or Puff.
Step 4Stars and Daily ArenaEach arena has three stars on its goal card. The Daily Arena is the same map for everyone each day.
