Clackabout

Drive a wind-up toy truck across 100 toy-box obstacle courses and swap into a boat, rocket car or digger mid-run to reach the flag.

GenreToy car physics puzzlePuzzle

Daily modeDaily Toy BoxNew one every day

Clackabout is a physics driving puzzle set inside a giant toy box. Every level is a little diorama built from lacquered wooden blocks, bookshelves, bathtubs, train tracks, kitchen tables and pillow forts, and your job is simple: get your wind-up toy to the checkered flag without flipping it, sinking it or dropping it off the table.

The twist is the swap gate. Drive through one and your toy pops into a different toy in the middle of the run, keeping its speed: the grippy Stomper monster truck climbs anything, the Pinewood racer is fast but slippery, the Tubtug boat floats and paddles across the bath, the Zoomrod rocket car burns fuel to fly over gaps, the heavy Scoopy digger shoves blocks out of the way, and the Boingo spring buggy charges up and hops. Reading each course and timing every swap is the puzzle.

There are 100 hand-built levels in ten worlds, from the Playroom Rug to the dark Attic Toy Chest, each with three stars that are explained before you start: reach the flag, beat the par time, and grab the gold sticker that hides off the fast line. Stars and coins unlock new worlds, paints for every toy, drivers and finish-line effects on the Toy Shelf. The Daily Toy Box gives everyone the same new course each day with two or three swaps, and keeps your day streak.

Crashes are part of the fun: every crash plays back instantly in slow motion, and you can retry in under a second or share the crash as a flipbook card. Finish a level and you see where you stand, with your rank, percentile and the next rank to beat, then send a challenge link so a friend races your exact ghost, verified by replaying it on their device. Everything works offline, progress is saved on your device, and leaderboard numbers are marked as preview until online ranks launch.

Step 3Three starsReach the flag, beat the par time and grab the gold sticker on each of 100 levels in ten toy-box worlds.
