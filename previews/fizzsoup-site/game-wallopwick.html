Wallopwick

Launch a floppy stick hero down 24 cartoon stunt courses. Bonk, flip and bounce off ramps, trampolines, cannons and cars to chain giant combos.

Wallopwick is a slapstick physics stunt game starring a bendy stick hero called Wick. Pull back, aim and let go: a shove, a cannon blast, a runaway car or a long drop sends Wick tumbling down a hand-built stunt course full of stairs, ramps, trampolines, bumpers, gongs, crates, spinning beams and swinging wrecking balls. Every bump scores. Harder hits score more, head bonks count double, and hits in quick succession build a combo multiplier of up to four times. Flips, air time, toppled crates, rung gongs, rings flown through and a landing in the target bin all add to the score.

The bonks are pure cartoon. Nobody gets hurt: there is no blood and nothing comes apart, only comic stars, squash and stretch, dizzy eyes and big "BONK!" bursts. The best hit of every run plays back in slow motion so you can enjoy the moment.

Wick is a proper ragdoll with bending joints, so no two runs are quite the same, yet the physics are fully deterministic. The same launch on the same course always plays out the same way, which keeps every attempt fair and lets you tune a shot until it is perfect.

There are 24 courses across four worlds, from the Backyard and the Building Site to the Big Top and the City Rooftops. They start gentle and get trickier. Each course has three stars: two score goals and a special stunt such as knocking over every crate, ringing the gong, landing two flips or dropping into the bin. Goals are shown on a goal card before you launch, tracked live on screen and explained on the results screen, which always waits for you. One star opens the next course.

The Daily Stunt is a fresh generated course every day, the same for everyone, with its own goals and a streak to keep. Eight original stick characters and eight hats unlock as you earn stars, score career points, land flips and play daily. Share your result as an image card, or send a challenge link that drops a friend onto the same course with your score to beat. Links carry everything they need, so nothing is uploaded.

Retrying takes a single tap. Stars, best scores, unlocks and your streak are saved on your device, and the game works offline. It plays in portrait or landscape on phones, tablets and desktop, with touch, mouse or keyboard.
