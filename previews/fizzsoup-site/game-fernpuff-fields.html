Fernpuff Fields

Drag two matching meadow friends together to grow them through 12 tiers, earn coins even while away, and fill your friend book across four seasons.

GenreMerge gardenMerge
OrientationAnyPhone, tablet, desktop
Daily modeNoneFree play only

Fernpuff Fields is a cozy idle merge game about growing a meadow full of tiny friends. Tap the seed bag to plant a sprout, then drag two identical friends onto each other and watch them pop, squash and stretch into the next creature: a Dewdrop Seed becomes a Peep Sprout, then a Clover Puff, and twelve tiers later a glowing Spring Keeper. Every friend on the board makes coins every second, and bigger friends make far more, so each merge makes your meadow richer.

Spend coins on more seeds and on three garden upgrades: Better Seeds plant higher-tier friends, the Sprinkler plants free friends on a timer, and the Lucky Clover sometimes makes a merge jump two tiers. Quick merges build chains for bonus coins, and a wandering Wishpuff floats across the meadow now and then for an extra payout if you tap it.

There are four seasons, each with its own line of twelve species. Spring starts with bunnies and lambs, Summer brings froggos and ladybugs, Autumn has leaf piles to rake and foxes to find, and Winter hides gifts under snowdrifts. Every season opens with a goal card and three stars to earn: grow the goal friend, do it within the meadow time limit, and finish all four season quests first. Your progress shows in the HUD, and a results screen explains every star you earned or missed. Earning a star unlocks the next season.

Every first discovery gets its own reveal card and a page in the Friend Book, which collects all 48 creatures with names and little stories. Stars, discoveries and daily streaks unlock hats and board themes. The Daily Bloom Rush is a 75-second merge sprint on a board seeded by the date, with a streak and a saved best score.

Your friends keep earning while you are away (at half rate, for up to three hours), and a welcome-back panel shows what they made. Progress saves automatically on your device. No account or download needed. Plays one-handed on a phone or with a mouse and keyboard on desktop, in portrait or landscape.
