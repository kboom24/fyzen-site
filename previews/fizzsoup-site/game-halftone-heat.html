Halftone Heat

Start last, finish first: overtake sticker cars, slipstream, boost and cut corners on 24 risograph-printed tracks, then race your friends' ghosts.

GenreArcade car racingRacing
Best onAny screenPhone, tablet, or computer
Daily funDaily TrackA new one every day

Halftone Heat is a fast, bright arcade car racer that looks like a fluorescent Risograph print: flat spot inks, halftone dots and slightly misregistered plates on warm cream paper. You start every race last in a pack of six chunky sticker cars, with a halftone ghost of the next driver to beat on the global board. Three laps, about 40 seconds, and the throttle is automatic, so all you do is steer and boost.

On a phone, drag anywhere with one thumb to steer; tap or swipe up to boost. On a keyboard, steer with the arrows or A/D, brake with Down or S and boost with Space, Shift, Up or W. Every overtake, near miss and boost pad tops up your boost meter. Tuck in behind a rival to slipstream for extra speed, cut across the dirt shortcuts on the inside of big corners (mind the cones), and ram a rival while boosting for a takedown.

There are 24 tracks across six printed locations: Citrus Coast, Paper Mesa, Cherry Hills, Blue Glacier, Night Market and Ember Ridge. Each track has three stars that are explained before you race and tracked live: finish on the podium, win the race, and a skill goal such as a target time, near misses, shortcuts, slipstream time, a takedown or boost pads. Stars and ink earned from racing unlock eight cars and twelve riso paints in the Garage. The Daily Track gives everyone the same new course each day, with a day streak.

The results screen shows where you stand: your rank, how many drivers you beat in percent, and the next rank to beat, for every track and the daily. Share a printed result card and a 'beat my time' link: your friend races the same track against your ghost. Everything works offline and your progress is saved on your device; leaderboard numbers are marked as preview until online ranks launch.
