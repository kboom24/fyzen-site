Daubstorm

Splat paint, not bullets! Team up with a buddy bot and cover more of the arena than your rivals in quick 60-second twin-stick matches.

GenrePaint battleAction & Arcade
Best onAny screenPhone, tablet, or computer
Daily funDaily DaubA new one every day

Daubstorm is a bright, family-friendly paint arena game. You are a little ink drop with a blaster full of colour, and your team has one minute to cover more of the arena than the other side. Nothing gets hurt: a rival who takes too much paint just goes splat, pops back at base and jumps straight back into the fight.

Every match is 2 against 2. You and your buddy bot face two rivals across a hand-drawn arena of hedges, crates, piers and ice. Paint the floor in your colour and refill your ink by standing in your own paint. Fill the storm meter to unleash a Daubstorm, a huge burst that soaks the ground around you. A live bar shows the score for both teams, and a mini-map shows who owns which corner.

There are 24 levels across four seasons: Petal Park in spring, Sunny Pier in summer, Leafy Yard in autumn and Frosty Rink in winter. Rivals get smarter as you go, and every sixth level is a longer boss match. Each level has three stars, and each star says why: win the match, reach a team-paint goal, or hit a personal target such as splats, paint share or a Daubstorm. The goal card shows all three before you start, the HUD tracks them live, and the results card explains which you earned.

A new Daily Arena arrives every day with a seeded map and blaster that are the same for everyone on that date. Play daily to grow your streak. Stars unlock new blasters (Spray Fan, Lobber and Roller), hats for your drop and new paint colours in the Locker.

Share a colourful result card with a challenge link. It opens the same arena for a friend and shows your score as the one to beat. Play with twin touch sticks, mouse and keyboard, or Apple Pencil, in portrait or landscape. Turn on Auto-paint to have the game aim and fire for you while you steer. Progress is saved on your device, and the game works offline.
