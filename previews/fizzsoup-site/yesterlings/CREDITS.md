# Yesterlings — credits

- **Game design, code, art and audio:** FizzSoup | Game Builder 3 for FizzSoup.
  - All art (heroes, Yesterlings, foes, the Hearthlight, backgrounds, logo, share card) is drawn in code on canvas (`art.js`).

  - There are no image, audio or font files of our own.
- **Engine:** Hexan `hexan.js` 1.2.1 (unmodified), with FizzSoup brand assets from `engine/brand/` (splash, badge, brand fonts under their OFL licences, copied by the build).
- **Third-party assets:** none.
