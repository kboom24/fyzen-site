Lumenhook

Drop a glowing lure into the deep, dodge fish on the way down, hook them on the way up, then upgrade and dive deeper.

GenreFishingAction & Arcade
Best onAny screenPhone, tablet, or computer
Daily funDaily DiveA new one every day

Lumenhook is a one-handed fishing game about the light at the end of your line. Cast your lure off a little wooden boat and steer it as it sinks: on the way down every fish you touch ends the dive early, so you weave through schools, rays and jellies to reach the end of your line. Then the reel kicks in and everything flips. Now you steer into the fish, stacking them on your hook in a wriggling, glowing bundle while you avoid the stinging jellies and spiky puffers that knock your catch loose.

Every haul is sold back at the boat for coins. Spend them on three upgrade tracks: a longer Line to reach new depths, a bigger Hook to carry more fish (plus a guard charm that forgives a bump on the way down), and a better Boat that sells for more and reels faster. Five zones wait below, from the Sunlit Shoals and the Kelp Cathedral through the Twilight Drop and the pitch-dark Midnight Reach to the glowing Abyss, each with its own creatures, light and music.

Twenty hand-built contracts give every cast a goal, with up to three stars for filling your hook, diving clean or landing a rare. The Daily Dive gives everyone the same seeded sea for the day with a featured rare worth triple, and keeps a streak with bonus coins. Twenty species fill your album, each with its own art and notes, and you can unlock new lures and boat paints along the way. Progress, coins, best haul and streak are saved on your device automatically.
