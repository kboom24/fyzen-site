Daylace

A brand-new painted jigsaw every day. Real interlocking pieces that click into place, 60 album pictures, stars, streaks and challenge links.

EngineHexan.jsFizzSoup in-house engine
GenreDaily jigsawPuzzle
OrientationAnyPhone, tablet, desktop
Daily modeToday’s jigsawNew one every day

Daylace is a jigsaw puzzle that paints a brand-new picture for you every day. Each picture is created on your device from the date: misty mountain mornings, harbours full of boats, glowing city skylines, kitchen tables with fruit and teapots, patchwork quilts and valleys full of hot-air balloons, at dawn, midday, sunset, dusk or under the stars. No two days look the same, and everyone in the world gets the same picture on the same date.

The pieces are real jigsaw pieces with knobs and holes that fit only one way. Pick one up from the table, drag it to the board and it clicks in with a little sparkle when it is in the right spot. Use Edges to sort corners and border pieces to the front, Peek to see a faint copy of the picture on the board, and the box lid on the goal card to study the picture before you start. On twist levels some pieces are turned; tap a piece to turn it the right way up.

The daily puzzle grows through the week, from a gentle 4x4 on Monday to a big 6x6 on Friday and Saturday and a Sunday Twist with turned pieces. Solve it every day to build your streak. Six albums hold 60 more pictures, from 3x3 warm-ups to a 7x7 finale, each unlocking the next.

Every puzzle has three stars, explained on the goal card, in the HUD and on the results screen: one for finishing the picture, one for beating the par time, and one for finishing within the move target without a hint. Stars unlock new table cloths: walnut, linen, night sky, cork and velvet. Share your finished picture as an image with a challenge link that gives a friend your time to beat. Works with fingers, mouse, trackpad and Apple Pencil (with palm rejection), plays offline and saves progress on your device.
