Gnawnauts

Grow a swarm of tiny gnawnauts that nibble cookies, toys, cars, skyscrapers and planets apart, bite by bite. Upgrade, molt and feast daily.

EngineHexan.jsFizzSoup in-house engine
GenreIdle munchingRelaxing
OrientationAnyPhone, tablet, desktop
Daily modeDaily feastNew one every day

Gnawnauts is an idle swarm game about very small creatures with very big appetites. Your nest sends out a stream of gnawnauts that fly to the object in front of them, bite off a piece and carry it home as bits. Every crumb they bring back is money for upgrades: Swarm Size adds more gnawnauts (hundreds of them fill the screen), Wing Speed shortens every trip, Jaw Strength makes each bite bigger and Zap Power makes your own taps hit harder. Tap the object to zap it yourself, catch the golden motes that drift by, and trigger Feeding Frenzies to speed the whole swarm up.

Five worlds grow from a picnic blanket to a toy box, a main street, downtown skyscrapers and finally deep space, each with six objects and a boss. A goal card explains each world before you start, the HUD shows your progress, and every world awards up to three stars: clear it, beat the par time, and catch enough golden motes. When the run slows down, Molt: start over with amber that permanently boosts every future swarm. Your gnawnauts keep gnawing while you are away, and a welcome-back screen shows what they earned. There is also a seeded 60-second Daily Feast with a streak, milestones with rewards, and unlockable swarm skins. Progress and settings are saved on your device, the game never advances on its own, and the sound toggle is always one tap away.
