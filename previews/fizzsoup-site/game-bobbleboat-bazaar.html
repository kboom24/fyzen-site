Bobbleboat Bazaar

Run a floating river market: walk your otter around, stock stalls, serve customers, hire a mole crew and fill trader-boat orders.

GenreIdle marketRelaxing
OrientationAnyPhone, tablet, desktop
Daily modeDaily RushNew one every day

Bobbleboat Bazaar is a cosy arcade-idle shop game set on a floating market in the middle of a lazy river. You are Ottilie the otter, and the market is yours to grow. Walk to the Berry Bush to pick a stack of bobbleberries, carry them to the basket, and watch ducks, frogs, bunnies and geese hop off the little round bobbleboat ferry to grab what they want. Ring them up at the till, scoop the coins off the counter and stand on a glowing tile to spend them: every tile opens something new, like a Lily Kettle for tea, a Bun Oven, a Melon Raft, a Lantern Loom or a Pearl Diver, bigger baskets, faster feet, a trader pier and lots of bunting.

When the market gets busy, hire the mole crew. A stocker mole in a yellow cap keeps one stall's basket full, and a cashier mole in a pink cap runs the till so you can rush around. Trader boats moor at the pier with an order board; bring them exactly what they ask for before the timer runs out for a big tip.

There are four islands, from Lily Landing to Lantern Lagoon, each with more goods and more tiles. Every island has three stars, and each one is explained up front: open every stall, finish within the par time, and fill the trader boat orders. The results card shows what you earned, your shells and where you stand today, and it waits until you choose Next island, Replay or Share. Shells unlock eight shopkeepers in the Wardrobe, including Cappi the capybara, Rusty the red panda and Lulu the axolotl.

The Daily Rush is a 90-second sprint in a fully built market that is the same for every player that day. Race your own ghost from your best run, keep your streak alive, restart instantly, and send a beat-me link that loads the same market and your ghost for a friend. You can also share a postcard of your market with a visit link.

Your cashier keeps the market open while you are away and earns at half speed for up to four hours. Optional videos can double your shells or your welcome-back coins; they are never required. Progress saves automatically on your device, and the game plays in portrait or landscape on phones, tablets and desktop with a one-thumb joystick, tap-to-walk, mouse or keyboard.

Step 1WalkDrag anywhere for a one-thumb joystick, or tap a spot to walk there. On desktop use WASD, the arrow keys or the mouse.
Step 2Stock and serveWalk into a stall to pick up stock, drop it in the basket, then serve customers at the till and scoop up the coins.
Step 3GrowStand on a glowing tile to buy it: new stalls, bigger baskets, faster feet and a mole crew who stock and serve for you.
Step 4StarsEach of the four islands has three stars: open every stall, beat the par time and fill trader boat orders. The Daily Rush is a 90-second sprint, new every day.
