Slipcrest

Hold to drift, let go to straighten: slide a tiny car along an endless zigzag road over the void, kiss the edges and chase the perfect line.

GenreCar driftingRacing
Best onAny screenPhone, tablet, or computer
Daily funFree playPlay anytime

Slipcrest is a one-tap drift game about the perfect line. Your little car races along a zigzag road floating over an endless void. Hold anywhere to throw it into a smoky drift through the bend, and let go to snap straight again. Release at exactly the right moment for a Perfect, which builds your combo multiplier. Slide close enough to brush the kerb for an Edge Kiss bonus. Leave it too late and you sail off the edge. Every second of drifting scores points, coins sit on the racing line, and the road keeps speeding up and tightening the further you get, so each run is a short, tense fight for one more corner.

Free Run is the endless mode. Survive as long as you can while the world drifts through four hand-built themes: Neon City, Desert Sunset, Snow Peaks and Deep Space. Each has its own weather, skyline and procedural soundtrack. Routes are 24 hand-tuned roads with a finish line and up to three stars each. A goal card before every route shows exactly what each star needs, chips on screen track your progress live, and the results screen explains what you earned and what you just missed. Daily Road is a fresh seeded road every day, the same for everyone, with three goals and a streak that grows each day you play.

Coins unlock ten original cars in the Garage, from the plucky Pebble hatch to the Nebula Nine. A first-run lesson with slow-motion prompts teaches the timing in seconds. Restarting is instant. Your best score, farthest distance, stars, coins, cars and streak are saved on your device. The game never moves on without you, and works in portrait or landscape with mouse, touch or keyboard.
