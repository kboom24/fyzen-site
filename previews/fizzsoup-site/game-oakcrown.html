Oakcrown

Chess and checkers in one cosy wooden set: play a computer at your level, solve a daily puzzle, or pass the device to a friend. Big pieces, plain-word hints.

Oakcrown puts two classics on one felt table: chess and checkers, carved in warm wood and polished brass, with pieces big enough to read at arm's length. Tap a piece, tap where it should go, and you're playing. Dragging works too, but you never need it.

Play the computer on a six-step ladder for each game, from Beginner, who still misses the odd checkmate, through Easy, Medium and Hard to Expert and Master, who think for a few seconds and play genuinely strong chess and checkers. Every level has three stars: win, win without Undo, and win without a Hint. Your rating climbs as you win and shows how you compare with the levels around you. The computer thinks in the background, so the board never freezes, and nothing is ever timed.

Stuck? Ask for a Hint and the coach draws an arrow and tells you why in plain words: "it captures a knight", "it puts the king in check", "it moves to a square where it can't be jumped". Undo as often as you like. Legal-move dots, clear messages such as "Your turn", "Check!" and "You must capture", a high-contrast mode and a gentle piece speed keep everything calm and readable.

Each day brings one new chess puzzle and one checkers puzzle, the same for everyone. Keep your daily streak alive, beat your best solve, and send a friend a "solve this" card with a link that opens the very same puzzle. Every chess puzzle is a checkmate in one to three moves, and each one has been checked by computer to have only one winning line. The Puzzle Path adds 30 chess and 30 checkers puzzles to work through in order.

Two Players mode is made for a tablet on the table between you. Choose whether the board turns around after each move or stays put.

The rules are the full rules. Chess has castling, en passant, promotion, stalemate, threefold repetition, the fifty-move rule and insufficient material. Checkers uses the American and English rules: captures are compulsory and multi-jumps must be finished.

Stars unlock new boards (Parlour Green, Rosewood Salon, Brass & Ebony, Moonlit Slate) and piece sets, and you can try a locked style for a game. Your stars, ratings, streak and settings are saved on your device.
