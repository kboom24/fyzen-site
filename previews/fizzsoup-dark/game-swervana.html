Swervana

Action & Arcade
1P · Touch 1P · Touch

Swerve, eat glowing orbs and grow into the longest snake in the arena. Boost to cut rivals off and make them burst. Climb the live top 10.

About the Game

Swervana is a snake arena game about the clever swerve. You start as a tiny ribbon of light in a round arena full of rival snakes. Eat glowing orbs to grow longer, and watch your name climb the live top-10 board at the side of the screen. Your head is fragile: touch another snake's body or the arena wall and your run ends. Your body is a weapon: swing it across a rival's path and they crash into you, bursting into a trail of big, juicy orbs for you to gobble up. Hold Boost to surge forward and slip ahead of someone, but every second of boost spends your length and sheds orbs behind you, so speed is always a trade.
