# Credits — Thistlewhirl

- Game design, code, art, sound and music: original work written for FizzSoup on the Hexan Engine.
- Engine: Hexan Engine `hexan.js` (hx@1.2.1), original, copied unmodified.
- Art: everything (island, thistle, the eight critters, huts, wares, icons, skies, the logo and the store thumbnails) is drawn procedurally on canvas in `js/art.js`. No image files.
- Sound and music: synthesized at runtime with WebAudio through `HX.Audio` (`js/audio.js`): pentatonic tap pops, chimes, a music-box lullaby. No sample files.
- Fonts: the platform brand fonts Nunito and Fredoka (Latin WOFF2 subsets, SIL Open Font License 1.1), bundled locally by the engine build into `brand/fonts/` with their licence texts. Nothing is downloaded from outside.
- FizzSoup brand splash, badge and favicons: platform brand kit (engine/brand), added by the build.
- Third-party assets: none.

Name check (web search, 2026-10-28): "Thistlewhirl" / "Thistle Whirl" turned up only as a fantasy-name idea, with no game, app or product. Rejected because they were taken: Mossmerry, Puffbloom, Dewdrop Hollow, Nibblenook, Cloudcrumb, Glimmerbough.
