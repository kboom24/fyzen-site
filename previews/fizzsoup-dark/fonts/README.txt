  Bold Italic (700 italic) and ExtraBold Italic (800 italic): UI, body, and the

- Fredoka SemiBold (600) and Bold (700): rounded wordmark only.

- Nunito Regular (400), Bold (700) and ExtraBold (800) remain in this folder for
