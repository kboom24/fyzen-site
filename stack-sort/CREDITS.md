# Credits: Chromacomb

Chromacomb is an original hex stack-sorting puzzle made for Playtoom.

- Code: original, written for this game (`game.js`, `core.js`). Engine: Hexan Engine (`hexan.js`), original and shared by Playtoom games.
- Game name, title logo and all in-game art: original, drawn procedurally on canvas at runtime. The game ships no image files of its own.
- Sound effects and music: original, synthesized at runtime with WebAudio. No sample files.
- Fonts: Playtoom platform fonts Nunito and Fredoka (Latin WOFF2 subsets, SIL Open Font License 1.1), bundled locally by the engine build into `brand/fonts/` with their licence texts; system UI fallback. Nothing is downloaded from outside.
- Playtoom splash, badge and favicon: platform brand assets provided by the engine build (`brand/`), owned by Playtoom.
- Mechanic: the general "sort stacks of hex tiles by color" puzzle genre. No names, art, sounds or code were taken from any other game.
- Third-party assets: only the OFL fonts above (via the engine). No other third-party assets.
