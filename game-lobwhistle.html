Lobwhistle

Swipe to smash, curve and lob your way past seaside animal rivals in breezy rooftop tennis.

EngineHexan.jsFyzen in-house engine
GenreSwipe tennisSports
OrientationAnyPhone, tablet, desktop
Daily modeSeaspray ServeNew one every day

Lobwhistle is rooftop tennis at a sunny seaside resort. Your fox runs to the ball on its own. You only swipe: direction aims the shot, length sets how deep and how hard it flies, and a curve in the swipe puts spin on the ball so it bends and kicks. Glowing tiles on the far court fill a Fever meter. Fill it and your next swipe is a Whistle Shot, a faster spiralling trick shot. Five courts change the bounce: Promenade plays true, Clay Terrace sits up and slows down, Frost Pier skids, the Boardwalk springs, and Whistle Point adds a cross-wind you can read on the windsock. Each animal rival has a signature serve. Win the game, string a long rally, and land tiles in a row to earn three stars. Each day Seaspray Serve, the Lighthouse Open, deals the same seeded three-rival ladder to every player, and your best score is kept on this device. Portrait and landscape both play with one finger, a mouse drag, or the keyboard.

Step 1Swipe to hitYour fox runs to the ball by itself. Swipe up to hit: the direction aims, the length sets how deep and hard, and a curve adds spin. A short tap is a safe shot.
Step 2Fill the Fever meterLand the ball on the glowing tiles on the far court. Six hits fill the Fever meter and your next swipe is a Whistle Shot, a fast spiralling trick shot.
Step 3Five courts, animal rivalsEvery court bounces differently, from the slow Clay Terrace to the skiddy Frost Pier and the windy Whistle Point. Each rival has a signature serve.
Step 4Stars and Seaspray ServeWin, string a long rally and land tiles in a row for three stars. The daily Seaspray Serve gives everyone the same three-rival ladder each day.
