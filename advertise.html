Our ad rules

Built into the platform, not left to chance.

Never over gameplay

No ad, overlay or pop-up ever sits inside the game frame or covers controls.

Breaks only

Interstitials fire only at natural pauses the game reports (level complete, game over).

Frequency caps

Max 1 interstitial per 3 minutes and never twice in a row. No ad in the first session minute. [Caps TBC]

Always labeled

Every slot carries a visible “AD” label and its format. Rewarded ads say what you get.

Family-aware
