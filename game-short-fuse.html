Short Fuse

3D shipyard shooter: light the fuse, clear rogue robots across a 6-mission campaign, drive jeeps and tanks, and chase the daily challenge.

EngineThree.jsOpen-source 3D library
Genre3D shipyard shooterAction & Arcade
OrientationLandscapeBest turned sideways
Daily modeDaily ShipyardNew one every day

Short Fuse is a browser 3D shooter set in a shipyard at dusk. Rogue robots have taken the yard and your bomb fuse is already lit — every robot you destroy buys you more time. Play the 6-mission campaign through to the Foreman finale, train on the range, or jump into solo waves on six maps with four difficulty tiers.

Gun drops come in rarities you stash and equip; the store and daily missions keep a run going. Four operators, six weapons, jeeps and tanks, and a short story intro. Touch, mouse and keyboard all work. Multiplayer and global boards stay off on Fyzen for now (solo and daily only). Teen and up: this is a shooting game.

Step 1Move and lookWASD or left stick to move, mouse or right stick to look. On a phone, left thumb moves and right thumb aims; the gun fires on its own while you hold.
Step 2Shoot and blastClick or tap to fire. Q or the blast button spends your charged blast. Space jumps, Shift dashes, V changes vehicle camera.
Step 3Vehicles and lootWalk up to a jeep or tank and enter it. Pick up gun drops, energy cells and mission objects in the yard.
Step 4Campaign and dailyClear six story missions with star goals, or play the daily challenge that is the same seed for everyone that day.
