Swervana

Swerve, eat glowing orbs and grow into the longest snake in the arena. Boost to cut rivals off and make them burst. Climb the live top 10.

EngineHexan.jsFytoon in-house engine
GenreSnake battleAction & Arcade
OrientationAnyPhone, tablet, desktop
Daily modeNoneFree play only

Swervana is a snake arena game about the clever swerve. You start as a tiny ribbon of light in a round arena full of rival snakes. Eat glowing orbs to grow longer, and watch your name climb the live top-10 board at the side of the screen. Your head is fragile: touch another snake's body or the arena wall and your run ends. Your body is a weapon: swing it across a rival's path and they crash into you, bursting into a trail of big, juicy orbs for you to gobble up. Hold Boost to surge forward and slip ahead of someone, but every second of boost spends your length and sheds orbs behind you, so speed is always a trade.

The rivals are computer-controlled snakes, each with its own style. Some are cautious and steer wide of trouble, some farm orbs quietly, some are aggressive cutters that race to get in front of you, and some boost wildly, panic-turn or simply make mistakes. Nothing is online and there is no waiting: a match starts in a moment and works offline.

There are ten arenas to clear, from Sprout Ring to Crown Arena, each with more rivals and sharper play. Every match has three goals shown on a goal card and tracked live on screen: reach a length (this clears the arena and opens the next), reach a top place on the board, and cut off a number of snakes. The results screen shows exactly what you earned and what you missed, your rank history over the last ten matches and your best rank, and it never moves on without you. Daily Arena is a new seeded arena every day, the same for everyone, with its own goals and a streak. Near misses trigger a slow-motion "So close!" moment. Twelve original skins unlock as you earn stars, grow longer, cut off rivals and keep your streak. Share your result as an image card, or send a challenge link that drops a friend into the same arena with your score to beat. Your best length, best rank, match history, stars, skins and streak are saved on your device. Plays in portrait or landscape with touch, mouse or keyboard.
