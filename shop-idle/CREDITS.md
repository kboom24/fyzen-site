# Credits — Bobbleboat Bazaar

- Code: original, written for Hexan (game.js, js/*.js). Engine: Hexan Engine (`hexan.js` 1.2.1, unmodified), original.
- Mechanics inspiration: the arcade-idle "walk-around shop" genre (carry stock, fill shelves, serve at a till, step on unlock tiles, hire helpers). Only the genre mechanics are shared; every name, character, layout, drawing, sound, tune and line of code here is original.
- Art: watercolour-style characters, stalls, boats, items and water are drawn procedurally on canvas at runtime. No image files.
- Sound and music: synthesized at runtime with WebAudio (`HX.Audio`): original melodies per island, the Daily Rush and the title. No sample files.
- Fonts: platform brand fonts Nunito and Fredoka (Latin WOFF2 subsets, SIL Open Font License 1.1), bundled locally by the engine build into `brand/fonts/` with their licence texts; system UI fallback. Nothing is downloaded from outside.
- Third-party assets: none.

## Name search (2026-10-28)
- Working title "Bazaar Bustle" was dropped: it is already the title of published music tracks (DavidKBD, 2025; Colin Willsher, 2011), and "Bustling Bazaar" is an existing hidden-object game.
- "Ripple Mart" was rejected because it is a company name.
- "Bobbleboat Bazaar": no game, app or trademark found under this name. "Bobbleboat" only appears as an unrelated bobblehead nickname (a 2015 baseball giveaway and the 2025 Milwaukee "Deep Thought" boat bobblehead). Chosen as the final title.
- Re-checked 23:10 PDT: still no exact match. Nearest names are "Bubble Bazaar" (a small itch.io undersea shop game and a PilPili bubble shooter) and "BubbleBoat" (a 2025 game-jam runner). They are different words and none is trademarked, so the title stands.
