# Credits — Wrinklewit

- Game design, all 60 riddles and the five daily riddle generators: original, written for the Playtoom library. No puzzle was
  taken from Brain Test, Who Is?, Brain Out, Tricky Puzzle or any other game; tricks are built around this game's own
  "the screen is the puzzle" mechanics (movable question words, drag-the-question, wiggle/shake, hold, wait, two fingers, tilt).
- Code: original. Engine: Hexan Engine 1.2.1 (`hexan.js`), original, used unmodified.
- Art: every image is drawn procedurally on canvas at runtime in the game's own "ink-pop" style (art.js: thick boiling ink
  outlines, flat saturated colours, halftone dots). Mascot "Wink" and its hats are original. No image files.
- Sound and music: synthesized at runtime with WebAudio (`HX.Audio`). No sample files.
- Fonts: platform brand fonts Nunito and Fredoka (Latin WOFF2 subsets, SIL Open Font License 1.1), bundled locally by the engine
  build into `brand/fonts/` with their licence texts; system UI fallback. Nothing is downloaded from outside.
- Third-party assets: none.
- Name: "Wrinklewit" chosen after a name/trademark screen (NAME-CHECK.md); formal clearance recommended before launch.
