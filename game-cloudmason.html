Cloudmason

Tap anywhere to drop the sliding slab. Line it up, slice the overhang, chain perfect drops and stack a tower from the meadow to the stars.

EngineHexan.jsFytoon in-house engine
GenreTower stackingAction & Arcade
OrientationAnyPhone, tablet, desktop
Daily modeNoneFree play only

Cloudmason is a calm, hypnotic one-tap stacking game. A slab glides back and forth above your tower. Tap anywhere on the screen, click, or press Space to drop it. Whatever hangs over the edge is sliced off and tumbles away, so the tower slowly narrows. Line the slab up exactly and it is a PERFECT: it snaps into place with a ring of light, a sparkle burst and the next note of a rising melody. Land three perfects in a row and the slab grows back a little with every further perfect, so a great streak can rescue a skinny tower.

Pip, a small cloud in a builder's hat, floats beside the top of your tower. Pip cheers your perfects, winces at big slices and gets nervous when the tower gets thin. The camera eases upward as you climb through five skies: the flowery Meadow, the Cloud Sea, Sunset Heights with its birds and glowing sun, the shimmering Aurora Veil and finally the Starfield. The music builds with you: new layers join as you climb and as your streak grows.

Scoring is shown before you play and on every results screen: every floor is worth 10 points, a perfect adds 20 times your streak (up to x5), and every 10 floors pays a growing milestone bonus. Endless mode keeps your best height and score. 24 Sky Levels each set three goals: reach the summit, land a number of perfects and hit a perfect streak. The goal card explains them, chips in the HUD show live progress, and the results screen lists what you earned and missed. It waits for you and never moves on by itself. The Daily Tower gives every player the same slabs, speeds and patterns each day, with your best height and a day streak. Share a result card and a challenge link: your friend builds the exact same tower with a line showing the height to beat. Progress unlocks block skins such as Glass, Candy, Marble, Neon and Gilded, plus new sky themes. Everything works offline and your progress is saved on your device.
