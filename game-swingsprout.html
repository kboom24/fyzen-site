Swingsprout

Hold to grab a glowing flower bud, let go to fling! Swing Sprig through 24 meadow courses, an endless run and a fresh daily course.

EngineHexan.jsFyzen in-house engine
GenreSwing & flingAction & Arcade
OrientationAnyPhone, tablet, desktop
Daily modeDaily courseNew one every day

Swingsprout is a one-button swinging game about momentum. You play Sprig, a small round seedling with a stretchy sprout vine. Press and hold anywhere to latch onto the nearest glowing flower bud, swing through the arc, and let go at the right moment to fling yourself across the meadow. Release on the upswing for a Perfect fling and an extra burst of speed, chain swing after swing without touching the ground, and race the clock to the finish flag. New players start in Sprout School, a short hands-on tutorial with slow-motion hints that teaches holding, letting go, mushroom pads and spinning thorns; it can be skipped and replayed any time from the Courses screen.

The 24 hand-built courses span four worlds: Sunny Meadow, Amber Canopy, Dusk Hollow and Starlit Grove. Each world adds something new: bouncy mushroom pads, spinning bramble wheels, drifting buds, wilting buds that give way after a moment, dandelion speed rings, gusts of wind and floating thistles. Every course has three stars: reach the finish for the first, and beat two target times for the others. The goal card shows the times before you start, and the results screen explains which stars you earned and why. Checkpoint flags keep a mistake from costing the whole run, and restarting takes a fraction of a second.

Endless mode builds a course that gets harder the farther you swing, so you can chase your best distance. The Daily Course is the same seeded course for everyone each day; finish it to grow your streak. Collect sunseeds and stars to unlock nine skins for Sprig and five gadget vines, such as the Long Vine, the bouncy Spring Coil, the Glide Leaf and the Magnet Bloom. Progress, best times and unlocks are saved on your device automatically.
