Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 31, 2026 · Last updated: October 31, 2026 · Version 1.0

1. Who we are

Playtoom (playtoom.com) is a free browser games site run by PAYON NETWORK LLC, a Florida limited liability company ("Playtoom", "we", "us"). We are the company responsible for (the "controller" of) the personal information described here.

Mailing address: PAYON NETWORK LLC, 515 E Las Olas Blvd, 1301-L45, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33301, United States. Email: hello@playtoom.com.

This policy explains what we collect, why, who helps us run the site, how long we keep things, and the choices you have. We've tried to keep it short and plain. If anything is unclear, just email us.

2. The short version

  • You can play without signing up. We don't ask for your name, email or birthday to play.
  • When you press Play or open Profile, we create an anonymous guest player card so your progress can be saved to the cloud. It has a random ID, a fun player name, an emoji avatar and your game saves.
  • We count plays and play time with our own cookie-free analytics, so we know which games people enjoy. You can turn this off under Privacy choices.
  • Leaderboards show only a player name, avatar and score.
  • Email is optional, only for players 13 and older, and only used to send sign-in links.
  • Online play, in games that offer it, is only with a friend who shares a room code: no chat, no names, and room data is deleted soon after the match.
  • No ad tracking. We don't sell or share your personal information.
  • You can ask us to delete your data any time at hello@playtoom.com.

3. What we collect

Before you press Play or Profile

Browsing the site and reading game pages doesn't create any account. Your browser keeps a few things on your own device (see Cookies and local storage below). Like any website, our hosting provider sees basic technical data needed to deliver pages (see Technical data).

Your guest player card

When you press Play or open your Profile, we create an anonymous guest profile. It includes:

  • A random guest ID. It isn't linked to your name, email or device identifiers.
  • A player name. You pick it from generated suggestions. Players who say they are 13 or older can type a custom name, which goes through a kid-safe name filter and may be held for review.
  • An emoji avatar chosen from a preset list.
  • An age range you tell us: under 13, teen (13-17) or adult. We don't ask for your birth date. If you haven't told us, we treat you as under 13.
  • Game data: cloud game saves (levels, stars, best scores, settings), recently played games, play counts, days played, and favorites.
  • Scores you post to leaderboards, with the date, game version and basic details of the run, used to rank scores and catch cheating.
  • Account housekeeping: when your profile was created and updated, when your name was last changed, and moderation flags (for example a name held for review, or a ban for abuse).

Game analytics (first-party, cookie-free)

To learn which games people play and where they get stuck, the site records simple game events: page views, when a game starts and stops (and how long it was played), levels won or lost, and how our own house ads perform. Each event has the game and page name, a random session ID, and either your guest player card ID or a scrambled (hashed) random ID kept on your device. We don't record your IP address, browser type or device identifiers in analytics, we don't use cookies for it, and on your first visit nothing is sent until you tap or click on the page. No outside analytics company is involved. You can turn analytics off under Privacy choices below, and we also switch it off when your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal.

If you add an email (13+ only)

Players who say they are 13 or older can add an email address so they can keep their player card when they switch devices or clear their browser. We use it only to send you sign-in links and important account messages. We don't send marketing email. If we ever start a newsletter, it will need its own separate opt-in. Players under 13 can't add an email.

Playtoom Plus (when it launches, adults only)

We plan to offer an optional paid, ad-free plan called Playtoom Plus. Only adults can buy it. When Playtoom Plus launches, payments will be handled by Stripe. If you subscribe, we keep your plan, its status, its renewal or end date, and Stripe's subscription reference. We never see or store your full card number. Stripe handles payment details under its own privacy policy. Players under 13 never see prices or upgrade offers.

Online play with a friend (room codes)

Some games let two to four players play together online. One player creates a room and gets a short room code to share, and friends type it in. There's no matching with strangers. While a room exists we keep only the room code, the game and its version, a random game seed, the guest IDs of the players in it (one for each seat, up to 4, so only they can use the room) and the times it was created and last used. To stop abuse, we count room attempts using a scrambled (salted and hashed) code made from your network address; we don't store the address itself, and these counters are kept for about one day. Moves and preset emotes pass between the players through Supabase's realtime service and are never stored. There's no chat, no typed text and no player names in online play. A room closes when the players leave, after 30 minutes without play or after 2 hours at most, and is deleted about 1 hour after it closes.

Technical data

When your browser talks to our servers, our providers automatically receive technical information such as your IP address, browser and device type, and request times. We use this only to deliver the site, keep it secure, prevent abuse (for example rate limits on saves and score posts) and fix problems. We don't use it to build advertising profiles.

4. How we use it, and our legal bases

We use your information only to run Playtoom. If you're in the European Economic Area or the UK, the law asks us to name a "legal basis" for each use:

  • To run the games and your player card (saving progress, syncing devices, favorites, recently played): to provide the service you asked for (contract).
  • Leaderboards and daily challenges: to provide the service, and our legitimate interest in fair, fun competition.
  • Sign-in links by email: to provide the service you asked for (contract).
  • Security, anti-cheat, name filtering and moderation: our legitimate interest in keeping Playtoom safe, and legal obligations.
  • First-party analytics and fixing bugs: our legitimate interest in making Playtoom better, using the minimum data needed. You can object at any time by turning analytics off.
  • Playtoom Plus billing records (when it launches): to provide the subscription (contract) and to meet tax and accounting rules (legal obligation).

We don't use your data for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. What other players can see

Leaderboards are public. They show only your player name, avatar and score (and your rank). They never show your guest ID, email, age range or anything else about you. Pick a name that doesn't reveal who you are. That's one reason younger players choose from generated names.

6. Cookies and local storage

Playtoom doesn't use cookies for tracking or ads. It uses your browser's local storage (a small storage area on your own device) to:

  • save your game progress, settings, favorites, recently played games and play counts on this device;
  • keep you signed in to your guest player card (a sign-in token) and remember a few account basics such as your age range and whether you have Playtoom Plus;
  • remember site preferences, like text size, whether the menu is folded, that you've seen our privacy notice, and your analytics choice;
  • keep a random analytics ID and session ID (only scrambled before it reaches us), unless you turn analytics off.

This storage is essential for the site to work, so it's always on (the analytics IDs stop being used if you turn analytics off). Clearing your browser data removes it. If you have a cloud player card with an email, your saves come back when you sign in again; a guest card without an email can't be recovered after its sign-in token is cleared.

There are no third-party cookies and no advertising cookies on Playtoom.

7. Privacy choices

You can turn our first-party analytics off on this device at any time. Your games and saves keep working exactly the same.

Analytics setting: loading…

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a request to opt out of sale, sharing and targeted advertising (we don't do any of these), and we also turn analytics off.

8. Advertising

Playtoom is supported by advertising. Today the only ads on Playtoom are Playtoom's own house ads (for example for our games or Playtoom Plus), chosen by the game or page you're on. There's no third-party ad network, no ad cookies, no device advertising IDs and no advertising profiles.

Our ad rules: no ads over gameplay, breaks only at natural pauses, rewarded ads are always optional, and every ad is labeled. Players under 13, or players whose age we don't know, only ever see kid-appropriate ads, and never see Playtoom Plus offers.

If we ever work with an outside ad network, we'll update this policy first and ask for consent where the law requires it. Playtoom Plus subscribers don't see ads.

9. Kids and families (COPPA)

Playtoom is a general-audience site that children enjoy, and we've designed it with them in mind.

  • We don't knowingly collect personal information such as names, emails, phone numbers, addresses, photos or precise location from children under 13.
  • Guest play is anonymous. A child's guest card has only a random ID, a player name picked from generated suggestions, a preset emoji avatar and game data. We use the guest ID only to run the site (saving progress, leaderboards, security and our own analytics), never for advertising profiles or behavioral ads.
  • Children under 13 can't add an email, can't type a custom player name, and never see prices or upgrade offers.
  • Online play is friends-only, by room code. Children, and players whose age we don't know, can play online only with a friend who shares a room code with them. There's no chat, no typed messages and no player names, only preset emotes, and no matching with strangers.
  • If we learn that we've collected personal information from a child under 13 in a way COPPA doesn't allow, we'll delete it.
  • Parents and guardians can ask us to review or delete their child's data, or to stop further collection, by emailing hello@playtoom.com. Tell us the player name and the game or device used, and we'll help find the card.

10. Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share it only with service providers that help us run Playtoom, who may use it only to provide their services to us:

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in system. Your player card, saves, scores and analytics events are stored on Supabase servers in the United States (US West).
  • Cloudflare: hosts the website and delivers it quickly and securely around the world.
  • Resend: sends sign-in link emails.
  • Stripe: will process Playtoom Plus payments when Playtoom Plus launches.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect players' safety or our rights, or as part of a merger, sale or reorganization of the business (in which case this policy keeps applying to your data).

11. International transfers

Playtoom is run from the United States and our data is stored in the United States. If you play from outside the US, including from the EEA or UK, your information is transferred to and handled in the US. Where the law requires it, we rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK addendum) in our providers' data processing terms.

12. How long we keep it

  • Guest player cards with no email that haven't been used for 12 months are deleted automatically, along with their saves, scores and analytics events.
  • Player cards with an email are kept while you use them. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete it and its saves and scores within 30 days.
  • Leaderboard scores are kept with your player card and are deleted when the card is deleted.
  • Analytics events are kept for 13 months. After that we keep only daily totals per game (for example "120 plays on Tuesday"), which don't identify anyone.
  • Rate-limit counters used to stop abuse are kept for about one day.
  • Online play rooms (room code, the players' guest IDs, game, seed and times) are deleted about 1 hour after the room closes. Moves are never stored.
  • Service logs are kept for a limited period by our providers (typically under 90 days).
  • Playtoom Plus billing records (when it launches) are kept as long as tax and accounting laws require.
  • Local storage on your device stays until you clear your browser data.

13. Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you can ask us to see, correct, download or delete your information, and we'll respond to reasonable requests. Depending on where you live, the law gives you specific rights:

  • EEA and UK (GDPR / UK GDPR): access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdrawing consent at any time. You can also complain to your local data protection authority, or in the UK the ICO.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA) and other US states with privacy laws: to know what we collect and how we use it, to access, correct and delete it, and to opt out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising (we don't do these). We won't treat you differently for using your rights.
  • Global Privacy Control: we honor GPC signals as an opt-out (see Privacy choices).

You can also change your player name and avatar on your Profile, turn analytics off under Privacy choices, and remove local data by clearing your browser storage.

14. How to delete your account or data

  • Email hello@playtoom.com with the subject "Delete my data". Include your player name and, if you added one, the email on your account. We'll delete it and confirm within 30 days. We may ask a question to make sure the request comes from the player or their parent.
  • Coming soon: a "Delete my player card" button on your Profile page.
  • Parents can make requests for their children the same way.
  • Authorized agents can make requests for California residents, with proof that the player authorized them.

Deleting your card removes your profile, cloud saves, scores, leaderboard entries and analytics events. Billing records we must keep by law are kept separately until they can be deleted.

15. Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections (HTTPS), strict database rules that let each player read and change only their own data, limited staff access with a log of staff actions, rate limits and a kid-safe name filter. No system is perfectly secure, so if we ever learn of a breach affecting your information, we'll let you know as the law requires.

16. Changes to this policy

We review this policy from time to time and update it when our practices or the law change. We'll post the new version here with a new "last updated" date. If a change is significant, for example a new use of your data or an outside ad network, we'll let you know on the site before it applies and ask for consent where the law requires it.
