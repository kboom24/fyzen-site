Privacy

Preview notice: this is the Fyzen preview build. A full privacy policy will be published before launch.

What this preview does

This preview is a static website. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no cookies and no ads.

What stays on your device

Your saved games, recently played list, theme choice and in-game progress are kept in your browser's local storage on this device only. They are never sent to Fyzen.

Clearing it

Use "Clear my history" on Your Fyzen, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Hosting

The pages and games are served as static files by the hosting provider, which may keep standard server logs.
